Learning journey

Listening to AI communities around the world.

Alongside building Islas Tech, I've been attending AI events and meetups to see how different communities and governments are adopting AI and supporting local founders. Here are my most recent stops, followed by earlier events from 2026.

Dubai, UAE · October 2026

Dubai AI Festival 2026, as a delegate

The third edition of the Dubai AI Festival at Dubai World Trade Centre, organised by Dubai AI Campus at DIFC in partnership with the Office of the UAE Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence. Under the theme "Where Intelligence Meets Opportunity", it brought together 150+ speakers, 200 exhibitors and attendees from over 100 countries.

Sessions covered agentic AI, AI governance, investment and the future of work, with a focus on financial services, government and industry. I talked with exhibitors about AI and robotics, and connected with founders, investors and enterprise leaders.

Frankfurt, Germany · 29–30 October 2026

AI Week Frankfurt at THE SQUAIRE

Two days at Frankfurt Airport's THE SQUAIRE. Finance Day brought talks on AI in banking and financial services from Commerzbank, the European Central Bank and a panel of bank leaders, plus sessions on building AI agents with OpenAI and Cohere. That evening I joined the AI Week × AI Hub Frankfurt Meetup for networking, and the next day was the Builders and hackathon day.

A highlight: the organizers interviewed me about my experience of AI weeks around the world, from Taiwan, Malaysia and Singapore to Abu Dhabi, Dubai and Frankfurt.

Zurich, Switzerland · 4 Nov 2026

Mindstone Zurich AI Meetup

An evening of practical AI talks at Unit8 on Beethovenstrasse, on three ways into AI: for organisations, for creators and for builders.

Zurich, Switzerland · 5 Nov 2026

Paris, France · 7 November 2026

Hack the Feed: my first hackathon

An AI video hackathon run by Oriane, Lovable and Brut at Albert School Paris. Working solo, I built Chef Radar Paris in Lovable: it uses Oriane's live data to find rising Paris chefs on social media, then writes a Brut-style 60-second video story about each one.

I submitted it in Track 01 (Video Search) as team #5, and met Oriane's co-founder along the way.

What I'm building

Islas Tech: an AI co-founder for food creators.
