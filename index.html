My lens

I see fragmentation
and build clarity.

I spent eight years as the invisible co-founder of iSLAS Filipino BBQ & Bar — doing the finance, the permits, the payroll, the POS reconciliation, the supplier negotiations, the compliance, the events. All of it, while Marc ran the kitchen, while working full-time at Scotiabank in the early years, and while holding the emotional weight of a small business that our family had everything riding on.

That experience gave me a specific lens: I see where systems break before the people running them do. I see the fragmentation that leads to midnight spreadsheets, surprise penalties, and burnout. And I know how to design the clarity that replaces it — because I built it, imperfectly, for eight years before I had the tools to build it properly.

"The missing piece was never people or customers. It was a system that could hold everything the operator was holding in their head."

I completed the Rotman Executive Program in Generative & Agentic AI for Business in May 2026 — and I'm a CPA Ontario PREP candidate. My finance background spans Accenture (SAP FI/CO), Scotiabank, and The Daniels Corporation. All of it feeds directly into what I'm building. None of it is about getting a job. All of it is about building the system.

What I'm building

Islas Tech:
The invisible co-founder.

A circular economy connector for hospitality — using advanced technology to reduce waste, rebuild financial trust, and prove that independent operators are sustainable, investable, future-ready businesses.

The first product is the 100-Operator Data Sprint: 100 structured one-on-one sessions with small operators in the Greater Toronto Area, building the proprietary Operator Logic dataset that no competitor can replicate.

We are currently in Stage 02: Prove. Cohort 1 is open. 14 founders and counting. If you're a restaurant, bakery, café, pop-up, or food brand — your session helps build the system the whole industry needs.
